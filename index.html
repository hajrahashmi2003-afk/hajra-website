About Me
Integrative practitioner blending clinical expertise with spiritual insight

Clinical & Academic Background

I hold an M.Sc. in Clinical Psychology from Mount Carmel College, Bangalore, and am currently pursuing the Islamic Psychology & Counselling course at Al-Balagh Academy. My training includes evidence-based modalities: CBT, DBT, REBT, ACT, IFS, and trauma-informed approaches. I am experienced in working with marital and relational concerns, behavioural addictions, anxiety disorders, trauma-related distress, personality rigidity (including OCPD traits), and adjustment difficulties.

Islamic Integrative Framework

I conceptualize distress through the lenses of nafs (self-regulation & internal conflict), tazkiyah (gradual psychological growth), fitrah (innate orientation toward balance), and ruh (existential meaning). Drawing upon Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyyah's model of the self, I work with behavioural addictions and moral distress through compassion and accountability — not shame — while integrating contemporary psychology.

Culturally Sensitive & Palliative Care

I provide psychological support in palliative care settings, addressing anticipatory grief, caregiver burden, and end-of-life emotional distress. My work is collaborative, respectful, and guided by ethical responsibility — always attentive to each client's spiritual and cultural context.

Professional Ethos

Confidentiality, empathy, religio-cultural competence, and evidence-based practice form the foundation of every session. I work with individuals, couples, and families toward resilience, emotional regulation, and value-based meaningful living.
