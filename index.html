About Me
Integrative practitioner blending clinical expertise with spiritual insight

Clinical Background

I am a counselling psychologist with extensive training in evidence-based modalities including CBT, person-centred therapy, and trauma-informed approaches. My work is grounded in ethical practice, informed consent, and ongoing professional development.

Islamic Integrative Framework

I integrate Islamic concepts of nafs (self), tazkiyah (purification), and fitrah (innate disposition) within contemporary therapeutic models. This creates a space where clients can explore psychological concerns without separating their spiritual identity.

Culturally Sensitive Care

I honor the intersection of culture, faith, and mental health. My practice is inclusive, non-judgmental, and tailored to each individual's unique context, values, and goals.

Professional Ethos

Confidentiality, empathy, and evidence-based practice form the foundation of every session. I am committed to supporting individuals, couples, and families in their journey toward resilience and wholeness.
